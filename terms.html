Merchanting Glossary

Flipping
The act of buying an item for a low price and selling it for a higher price.
Margin
The difference between the Buy Price and the Sell Price.
GE Tax
A 1% fee removed from the seller's revenue on the Grand Exchange.
High Alchemy
A magic spell that converts items into gold, often setting the price floor for items.
