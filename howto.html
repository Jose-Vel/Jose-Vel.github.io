How to Find a Profitable Flip

Flipping is the art of buying low and selling high. Follow these steps to secure your first profit.

Step 1: Check the Margins

Before you commit your gold, you must "margin check" an item. Buy one item at a high price to find the instant-sell price, then sell it back low to find the instant-buy price.

Step 2: Calculate Your Potential Profit

Subtract the Buy Price from the Sell Price. Ensure the gap is wide enough to cover the 1% Grand Exchange Tax.

Step 3: Place Your Offers

Place a buy offer for the low price you discovered. Once it fills, list the items for the high price.
